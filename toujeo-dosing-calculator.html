Sanofi: Toujeo.com Dosing Calculator

A comprehensive insulin dosing calculator designed to help healthcare professionals determine optimal glycemic control strategies for patients using Toujeo Max SoloStar and SoloStar delivery systems.

About the Project

This digital tool was developed to streamline the complex process of insulin dosing calculations for healthcare providers. The interface needed to be precise, compliant with pharmaceutical regulations, and intuitive enough for quick clinical decision-making.

Working closely with medical advisors and the Sanofi team, we created a user experience that balances regulatory requirements with usability. The calculator guides users through a step-by-step process, ensuring accuracy while minimizing cognitive load during patient consultations.

Design Approach

The design prioritizes clarity and precision through clean typography, generous white space, and a logical information hierarchy. Interactive elements provide real-time feedback, while the stepped workflow prevents errors and ensures all necessary information is collected before generating dosing recommendations.
