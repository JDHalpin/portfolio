PRADAXA: AR / Rep-driven Experience

An innovative augmented reality experience designed for pharmaceutical sales representatives to deliver engaging, interactive product education to healthcare professionals.

About the Project

The PRADAXA AR experience reimagines how pharmaceutical sales representatives engage with healthcare providers during office visits. Moving beyond traditional sales materials, this immersive application uses augmented reality to demonstrate complex medical concepts in three-dimensional space.

As Senior UX Architect, I led the experience design for this cutting-edge sales tool. The challenge was creating an AR interface that sales reps could confidently navigate in real-time consultations while maintaining the scientific rigor required for pharmaceutical communications.

Design Approach

The experience centers on intuitive gesture controls and spatial navigation that feel natural even to first-time AR users. Sales representatives can seamlessly transition between different modules—mechanism of action, clinical data, patient profiles—using simple tap and swipe interactions.

Visual design balances scientific accuracy with engagement. 3D molecular models, anatomical visualizations, and data visualizations are rendered with medical precision but presented in ways that facilitate conversation rather than replacing it. The interface recedes when not needed, keeping the focus on the human interaction.

Extensive user testing with sales teams informed the final design. We observed real rep-HCP interactions to understand natural conversation flows, then designed the AR experience to support rather than interrupt those patterns. The result is a tool that enhances the sales conversation while maintaining professional credibility.

The project required close collaboration with medical, legal, and regulatory teams to ensure all AR content met pharmaceutical industry standards while leveraging the unique affordances of spatial computing.
