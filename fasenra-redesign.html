AMGEN: Fasenra.com Redesign

A comprehensive redesign of the Fasenra.com HCP (Healthcare Professional) website, reimagining the user experience and technical architecture to better serve medical professionals seeking treatment information.

About the Project

As UX Architect Lead, I spearheaded the complete redesign of Fasenra.com, focusing on creating an intuitive, accessible experience for healthcare professionals. The project required balancing complex medical information with regulatory compliance while ensuring the site met the highest standards of usability.

The redesign involved a ground-up rethinking of the information architecture, navigation patterns, and content strategy. We conducted extensive user research with HCPs to understand their needs, pain points, and workflows when researching treatment options.

Design Approach

The design system prioritizes quick access to critical clinical data while maintaining WCAG compliance and pharmaceutical industry standards. We implemented a modular component library that allows for flexible content presentation across different sections of the site.

The technical architecture was revised to improve performance, scalability, and content management workflows. This included implementing a new CMS structure and optimizing for mobile experiences without compromising the depth of information HCPs require.
