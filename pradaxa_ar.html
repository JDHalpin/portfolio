Experiential Design, AR/XR: Veeva CLM Sales Tool

An augmented reality sales tool for iPad that transformed standard KOL video content into an immersive, interactive experience for pharmaceutical sales representatives.

The Challenge

Boehringer Ingelheim had just issued 6th generation iPad Pros to their entire sales force and wanted an AR experience to mark the upgrade. The brief was open-ended: "build something in AR." My job was to turn that into a focused, compliant, and effective sales tool that reps could actually use in HCP conversations.

My Role

Senior UX Architect, responsible for competitive research, interaction design, wireframes, and KOL performance direction. I collaborated with creative on storyboards and guided the experience through regulatory review.

Research & Discovery

I audited existing AR experiences in the pharmaceutical and broader healthcare market, documenting interaction patterns, technical approaches, and engagement mechanics. I reviewed findings with the internal team and client to establish a shared understanding of what "good" looked like in this space.

Stakeholder interviews with the brand team clarified priorities: the experience needed to support the sales narrative, not distract from it. Reps needed something that enhanced their conversations with HCPs.

Key Design Decisions

1. Animated bar charts as a presentation mechanic
I advocated for animating data visualizations in AR, borrowing a pattern we'd used successfully on web to bring infographics to life. In an AR context, this gave the KOL something to gesture toward during the presentation. The animation created natural pauses and visual anchors that supported the sales narrative rather than competing with it.

2. KOL gesture choreography
The AR content would be triggered alongside a KOL video. I diagrammed specific gestures and motions the KOL could make, which the creative and production teams used to direct the performance. This ensured the human presenter and the AR elements worked together rather than feeling like separate layers.

3. QR-triggered activation
The experience launched when reps scanned a QR code on a print piece. This kept the barrier to entry low and tied the digital experience to existing sales materials, making it easy for reps to integrate into their workflow.

"This is amazing. It’s exactly what we wanted and more. The AR really brings the data to life in a way that will resonate with our reps and HCPs. I can already see this being a game-changer for our sales conversations."

- Senior Brand Client

Outcome

The brand team at Boehringer Ingelheim praised the execution. Engagement with the Veeva CLM platform increased approximately 20% year over year. For its time, this was an innovative application of AR in pharmaceutical sales enablement.

Reflection

This project reinforced that product design thinking applies beyond apps and websites. A talking-head KOL video is standard pharma creative. By applying interaction design principles, spatial UX, and user-centered framing, we transformed it into an immersive experience that actually held attention. The tools change; the discipline doesn't.
